# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Doing Process exists to value knowledge held by people the legal system often
ignores — including people who are incarcerated. Everyone who takes part, in any
role, deserves a respectful, harassment-free experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique ideas, not people.
- Protect contributor privacy. Never share, guess at, or try to re-identify a
  contributor's legal identity. Treat scanned material as sensitive.
- Give credit as contributors have chosen to receive it (handle, or none).
- Welcome newcomers and explain generously.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or demeaning comments about any person or group.
- Publishing others' private information, or attempting to de-anonymize a
  pseudonymous contributor.
- Using this project to demean, surveil, or endanger incarcerated people or any
  other group.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, and any
official channel — and to conduct that affects the community elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to **conduct@doingprocess.org**. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers who don't act in good faith may face consequences
decided by other maintainers.
